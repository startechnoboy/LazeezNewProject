Our Kitchen

From the tandoor
to your table.

Lazeez NewTown is an AC family restaurant tucked into the Shapoorji C Gate stretch of Action Area III — the kind of place regulars walk to for a quick roll and stay for a full spread. Every skewer you see hanging in our kitchen window is marinated in-house and grilled fresh through the evening, not held and reheated.

Behind the glass, our kitchen runs the way a family restaurant should: capped and gloved staff, an open charcoal grill, and a menu built around Kolkata's favourite Mughlai flavours.

  • Charcoal tandoor kebabs, grilled to order
  • Hygienic kitchen — hairnets, gloves, daily prep
  • Comfortable AC seating for the whole family
  • Dine-in, takeaway parcels & home delivery
